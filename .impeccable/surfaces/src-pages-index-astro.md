# Surface brief: ASM marketing site (home + service pages)

Scope: the whole public site; the home page is the primary surface. Visitor mode: **Persuade**.
Audience/job: NZ homeowners and commercial property owners choosing, automating or fixing a garage door or gate; act = call or request a quote.
Proof available: none supplied (no testimonials, ratings, credentials); representative stock photography, labelled. Constraints: no invented claims; placeholders live in `src/config/site.ts`.
Build path: code-led (no image generation in this environment).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is built on the sectional door. Every page is a stack of horizontal panels: full-width bands separated by machined groove lines, in two materials (ironsand steel and pale concrete), with bronze as the only hardware. It refuses the trade-site default: split hero beside a photo, orange accent, a row of equal icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: Ironsand near-black and basalt charcoal, concrete and limestone off-whites, one anodised-bronze accent used only for actions, active states, rules and small hardware details (icons). Archivo variable: expanded heavy cuts for display, normal width for reading. Square corners (2px max), photography edge-to-edge or in hard-edged frames, hairline grooves (dark line over light line) as dividers, uppercase tracked labels only on controls.

STORY: Visitor sees a premium entrance, understands in one line that ASM does garage doors, gates, automation and repairs; believes it from the photography, explained door/gate types and a clear process; then calls or requests a quote, prefilled by the service they were reading.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-bleed reel of four colourful service photographs (gates, garage doors, automation, repairs & servicing; the client asked for a brighter, service-focused hero). Display headline "Make an entrance." at the display maximum (6rem), bottom-left over a bottom/left scrim; one-sentence offer; bronze "Request a quote" + outline "Explore our services"; phone link. Bottom-right reel panel with caption, Explore link and four labelled progress tabs. Trust rail of four plain facts pinned to the bottom. On load, a stack of charcoal door panels lifts off the reel (the signature interaction); text is visible from the first frame.

FORM: Brief-pinned direction (architectural/industrial, charcoal/off-white/warm neutral/metallic accent). The concept roll was deliberately not run: a brief-pinned direction beats the roll. seed: none (not run, brief-pinned)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
